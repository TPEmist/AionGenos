## R-S6 — "Is the memory finding itself reach-specific?" (contact-rich generality)

**Anticipated objection (ICRA Reviewer 2, read-through surface 6).** The
LIBERO two-boundary anatomy (§5.3 / D11 Discussion) is a complete defense
of *why we stopped at reach* — it localizes both walls (contact-precision
ceiling on grasp+push; primitive-expressivity gap on twist) to the
control-stack / primitive-vocabulary layers, outside the paper's claimed
contribution, with the reasoning layer shown sound. But a reviewer will
push one step further: the anatomy proves the *interface* cannot yet reach
contact-rich tasks — it does **not** show the marginal/conditional memory
split would still hold there. So: is the headline finding reach-specific?

**Pre-armed response.** The anatomy establishes that this interface cannot
yet reach contact-rich tasks; whether the marginal/conditional split
generalizes there is an open empirical question — and precisely the first
measurement scheduled once closed-loop contact control lands (paper 2,
WP1). The claim in this paper is explicitly scoped to reach-family tasks
(L0a reach + L2 pose-reach, both position-goal), and we do not assert
generality to contact-rich manipulation; we identify it as the next
measurement, not an assumption. This turns "we don't know" into "we know
what we scheduled to measure" — defensive → forward-looking.

**Why this is honest, not a dodge.** The two-task evidence (L0a + L2)
already spans an easy/hard reach pair and the mechanism replicated in
direction on the harder one (n.s. in size); extending to a *different
control regime* (contact) is a genuinely different experiment, gated on a
control-stack capability the paper openly says it lacks (§5.3's two
unlocks: closed-loop contact control + expanded primitive vocabulary).
Claiming contact-generality now would be the overreach; scheduling it is
the disciplined move.
