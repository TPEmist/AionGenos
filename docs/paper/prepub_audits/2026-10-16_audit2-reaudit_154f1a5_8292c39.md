  fetched 2026-10-15):

    anonymized";

  TMLR text resolved to the named public repository `TPEmist/AionGenos`.
  Searching any of them identifies the authors.

  one source for the named and anonymous builds:

    in TMLR;

    camera-ready");
  - the single mention of the workshop version is hidden;
  - the PDF metadata is empty.
- **Why this route** rather than an anonymised mirror:
  - it keeps one source of truth;
  - the birth-certificate gate still binds every cited SHA to a real commit
    (`shas_resolve` reads the `\commit{…}` argument);
  - nothing has to be maintained outside the repository.
